# About
Xylem is a structured logging and distributed tracing framework designed to visualize the internal flow of your applications. Xylem traces the path of execution through your system—from the root request to the deepest service call—turning disconnected logs into a unified, hierarchical narrative.

# Why the Name?
Why "Xylem"? In botany, xylem is the tissue that transports water and nutrients from the roots to the leaves. In software, your data flows through deep, branching paths of execution. Xylem is the tool that makes that internal flow visible, ensuring you can always trace the health of your system from the root up.
